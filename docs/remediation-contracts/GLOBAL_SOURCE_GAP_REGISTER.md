## 1. Governing rule

This register records unresolved source/provenance gaps without converting uncertainty into either invention or deletion.

`SOURCE_NOT_RECOVERABLE_FROM_AVAILABLE_RECORD` or `SOURCE_EXHAUSTED` means the exact historical detail is not recoverable from the source basis actually checked. It does **not** prove the detail never existed and does **not** prove the recovered rule is wrong.

Where substantive semantics are conservative and uncontradicted but exact historical form is unproved, the audit may preserve the recovered rule while separately limiting the assurance claim.

## 2. Systematic Phase A recovery pattern — GAP-PATTERN-01

**Status:** OPEN / SYSTEMATIC SOURCE-RECOVERY LIMITATION  
**Affected nodes observed so far:** R4, R5, R6  
**Source basis:** same-continuity reviewer memory after deliberate targeted re-read  
**Independent source available:** NO

Three consecutive Phase A reviews independently reached source exhaustion on the same two structural categories:

1. **Per-node audit-structure provenance**
   - exact `A1` audit name/shape;
   - exact classification enum/family;
   - exact `M9+` discovered-defect numbering convention;
   - exact per-node provenance of the rule `AUDITED ≠ DEFECT FOUND ≠ DEFECT FIXED`.

2. **Fixture/closure-list exact-form provenance**
   - exact fixture letter range and order;
   - exact numbered closure-list count and order;
   - whether those exact enumerations were frozen in each node's original confirmation exchange versus synthesized during reconstruction from substantive rules.

### Interpretation

This repeated pattern is evidence of a **systematic limitation of same-continuity memory as a source**, not evidence that the repeated structures are false.

By the time R4–R6 were reconstructed/reviewed, the corpus-wide remediation convention was familiar enough that the reviewer could no longer reliably distinguish:

- genuine per-node historical recall; from
- later normalization/pattern transfer into a common audit template.

### Required treatment

- Preserve conservative recovered structures unless contradicted.
- Do not claim the exact historical form is T3-proven.
- Do not resolve the pattern through additional same-continuity memory passes alone.
- Include this pattern explicitly in the materially independent T4 packet.
- The T4 reviewer should check whether the same pattern appears in additional recovered nodes and whether underlying source material can distinguish node-specific origin from later normalization.
- If final governance chooses to retain an unproven structural convention because it is useful/safe, label it as **corrected-governance adoption**, not historical-source recovery.
