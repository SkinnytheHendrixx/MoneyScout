## 2. C13-01 — `R5 → R20`

**R5 pinned blob:** `dfa3534b7a62ae48f8dbc5104a382e2ca19b1929`  
**R20 pinned blob:** `d9d7788e4c5a8f4c0914cf845294b38386470333`

### 2.1 Relevant R5 source text

R5 states:

> **R5 × R20 — confirmation freshness at consequential boundaries.** A candidate may have been validly confirmed earlier, while the underlying authority/evidence/lifecycle state changes later. R5's confirmation is historical fact. R20 decides whether that confirmation remains sufficient at the next consequential boundary.

R5 further states:

> **R5 must not imply: CONFIRMED ONCE = AUTHORIZED FOREVER.**

R5 requires vocabulary compatibility on:

> **R5 ↔ R20:** agree on `confirmed_at`; confirmation fingerprint; authority fence; revalidation.

R5 defines the ownership split as:

> **R5 vs R20:** R5 records confirmed historical judgment. R20 governs future consequential adoption/revalidation.

R5's confirmation rule is material to consequential authority because:

> Only `CONFIRMED` permits canonical material closure.

and materiality includes:

> any conclusion later used as affirmative evidence for capital, Build, release, commercial, or other consequential authority.

R5's legacy rule further requires:

> Every pre-R5 material autonomous resolution is `LEGACY_UNCONFIRMED` by default.

and until qualifying treatment occurs:

> it may not serve as affirmative authority for a new consequential action.

### 2.2 Relevant R20 source text

R20's complete top-level consumed-node list names:

> - R3 current-enough evidence and freshness truth;
> - R4 exact Evaluation Cycle lineage;

> - R7 resource reservation/admission authority;

> - R19 complete immutable commercial lineage;

R5 is absent.

R20's required predicate families include:

> - authority/grant validity;
> - exact lifecycle state;
> - exact R7 resource/reservation eligibility;
> - exact R6/R18 capability readiness and binding validity;
> - R3 evidence freshness/current applicability where current-condition evidence is required;

R20's A0 representability audit enumerates exact identity failures such as missing provider/account binding, Offer Version/Grant, R9/R10 lineage, reservation scope, freshness applicability, adoption target, runtime epoch, and concurrency/multiplicity representation. ### 2.4 Classification

**Primary:** `MISSING_REQUIRED_COMPOSITION`  
**Tags:** `COMPOSES`, `OWNERSHIP_BOUNDARY`, `CERTIFICATION_DEPENDENCY`  
**Topology:** `UNILATERAL_DECLARATION`

`CERTIFICATION_DEPENDENCY` gate kind: **operational/integration**.

### 2.5 Adjudication rationale

R5 explicitly requires that a material autonomous conclusion be independently confirmed before canonical closure and explicitly states that R20 later decides whether that confirmation remains sufficient at a consequential boundary.

R20, however, contains no explicit predicate family, identity representation, boundary rule, A0 requirement, sibling-sweep item, or acceptance criterion for R5 confirmation.

No intentional mediator equivalent to C12-03's `R16 → R7 → R20` path exists here:

- R3 freshness cannot substitute for R5 independence;
- R4 lineage cannot substitute for R5 independence;
- R7 reservation cannot substitute for R5 independence;
- R11 owns challenge/successor routing, not successful independent confirmation.

Therefore the required composition itself is absent from the consuming boundary authority contract. This is not merely under-illustrated behavior or ambiguous wording.

This is the second confirmed `MISSING_REQUIRED_COMPOSITION` in Phase C after `R17 → R18`.

### 2.6 Required strengthening

Where a consequential boundary materially relies on an autonomous conclusion governed by R5, R20 must consume the exact qualifying R5 confirmation bound to that same candidate/evidence/lineage identity.

A valid lineage, fresh evidence, valid resource reservation, valid capability binding, valid artifact, or any other passing predicate cannot substitute for missing, challenged, inconclusive, stale, superseded, mismatched, or policy-incompatible independent confirmation.

### 2.7 Required acceptance fixture

The future remediation/certification fixture must prove at least:

1. Candidate X is material under R5.
2. Exact R4 lineage and R3 freshness are valid.
3. R7 reservation, R6/R18 capability, and all other applicable boundary predicates pass.
4. X remains only a `RESOLUTION_CANDIDATE` / equivalent unconfirmed candidate. R20 denies the consequential boundary.
5. X receives qualifying R5 confirmation bound to exact fingerprint F1. The same exact boundary may pass if every other applicable predicate passes.
6. Candidate/evidence/lineage materially changes to X2/F2. Confirmation F1 does not authorize X2/F2.
7. `CHALLENGED` and `INCONCLUSIVE` remain blocking for material closure/consumption.
8. `LEGACY_UNCONFIRMED` may not become current consequential authority merely because it historically drove state.
9. A nearby/same-topic/materially different candidate cannot borrow another candidate's confirmation.
10. R3, R4, R7, R11, schema validity, or other predicates may not be used as substitutes for R5 independent confirmation.
11. A candidate confirmed under one R5 confirmation-policy version does not automatically satisfy a later boundary evaluated under a materially different confirmation-policy version. Historical confirmation remains preserved, but reuse under materially changed policy requires the governed compatibility/revalidation rule rather than silent carry-forward.

The fixture must preserve the distinction between historical confirmation truth and current boundary eligibility.

### 2.8 Invalidation / re-review trigger

Any future endpoint amendment that introduces an explicit R5 confirmation predicate, mediator, or equivalent R20 representation must trigger re-review of this finding. Closure requires proving the new composition actually binds the exact candidate/evidence/lineage/policy identity rather than merely adding an R5 field or generic "confirmed" boolean.

## 3. C13-02 — `R7 → R20`

**R7 pinned blob:** `3a69868cbf5292fb7ba0aa4d1cc2891cebdce0bf`  
**R20 pinned blob:** `d9d7788e4c5a8f4c0914cf845294b38386470333`

### 3.1 Relevant R7 source text

R7 defines:

> ### R7 × R20
>
> R7 owns reservation. R20 owns whether a later consequential boundary may still consume/use that reservation under current authority, evidence, capability, lifecycle, lineage, and other predicates.
>
> R20 invalidation after a provider boundary cannot erase already-reserved/incurred exposure.

R7's vocabulary checkpoint includes:

> `reservation validity`, `authority validity`, `consumption`, `adoption`, `revocation after boundary`.

R7's ownership split states:

> **R7 vs R20:** resource reservation vs generalized consequential freshness fencing.

R7 also requires atomic linkage:

> R7 requires atomic linkage between the consequential Economic Action or equivalent authority record, its scarce-resource reservation set, and the exact execution attempt that may consume it.

and:

> either every required scope is reserved together, or none is.

### 3.2 Relevant R20 source text

R20 explicitly consumes:

> R7 resource reservation/admission authority.

Its dedicated R7 boundary states:

> ## 9. R7 boundary — resource authority must still be valid
>
> A resource reservation granted earlier does not permit execution if the governing authority facts required to consume it are no longer valid at the boundary.

R20 further states:

> R20 does not recreate R7 reservation arithmetic. It requires the exact reservation/authority state relevant to the action to remain eligible.

Potential disqualifiers include:

> - Master Mode / policy / scoped override changes;
> - reservation invalidation or exhaustion;
> - required aggregate scope no longer available;
> - provider/account/resource-pool mismatch;
> - authority lineage no longer matching the reserved operation.

And:

> R20 must not turn “reservation exists” into “dispatch is still legal.”

R20 E2E certification explicitly includes:

> `R7 × R20`

### 3.3 Classification

**Primary:** `CONSISTENT_CONSUMPTION`  
**Tags:** `CONSUMES`, `COMPOSES`, `OWNERSHIP_BOUNDARY`, `CERTIFICATION_DEPENDENCY`, `PARALLEL_NOT_MERGED`  
**Topology:** `BILATERAL_CORROBORATION`

`CERTIFICATION_DEPENDENCY` gate kind: **operational/joint-fixture**.

### 3.4 Adjudication rationale

The seam is explicitly owned and bilaterally described:

- R7 owns exact scarce-resource reservation and Economic Action/reservation/execution linkage;
- R20 consumes that exact R7 authority at the consequential boundary without re-performing R7 arithmetic;
- reservation validity and boundary eligibility remain separate predicates.

No ownership or authority laundering defect was found.

### 3.5 Targeted strengthening fixture

A lighter targeted fixture is required to make the exact-action identity seam executable:

1. Create action X with valid reservation vector `RX` across all applicable R7 scopes.
2. Create action Y with distinct valid reservation vector `RY` in superficially similar pools/scopes.
3. Both vectors are individually valid and have adequate headroom.
4. Invoke the R20 boundary for X while deliberately presenting/linking `RY` instead of `RX`.
5. R20 must fail closed because a valid reservation for another action is not reservation authority for X.
6. Matching Bet/provider/resource-class/current headroom or other pool-level similarity must not satisfy the exact-linkage requirement.
7. `RX` must remain tied to X's exact Economic Action/execution identity and all required scopes.
8. The fixture must also prove that one invalid/missing scope in `RX` cannot be hidden by validity of the remaining scopes.

This fixture strengthens exact-action-scoped consumption. It does not indicate a missing composition in the contracts.

### 3.6 Invalidation / re-review trigger

Any future R20 implementation/schema that consumes reservation state by pool identity, Bet identity, provider identity, or generic "reservation valid" status without preserving R7's exact Economic Action/reservation/execution linkage reopens this seam as a potential identity-substitution defect.

### 4.3 Classification

**Primary:** `CONSISTENT_CONSUMPTION`  
**Tags:** `CONSUMES`, `HARD_CHAIN`, `COMPOSES`, `OWNERSHIP_BOUNDARY`, `CERTIFICATION_DEPENDENCY`  
**Topology:** `UNILATERAL_DECLARATION`

`CERTIFICATION_DEPENDENCY` gate kind: **operational/integration**.

### 4.4 Adjudication rationale

R19 independently examines and consumes the R10 artifact/release segment in detail. R10 names R19 in the hard chain and E2E dependency set, but its detailed downstream semantic boundary is primarily R17 rather than an R19-specific boundary. Therefore the topology remains unilateral rather than bilateral.

The contracts nevertheless establish one exact-path mission and exact artifact lineage. No missing-composition classification is warranted.

### 4.5 Required lighter-tier identity-consistency fixture

Apply the same calibrated standard used for C11-01 and C12-02:

1. Create exact R10 Artifact Version / Release `P1`.
2. Create valid R17 Offer `O1` bound to `P1`.
3. R19 construction with direct R10 segment `P1` plus R17 segment `O1/P1` succeeds, assuming all other predicates pass.
4. Create valid `O2` bound to distinct Artifact Version / Release `P2`.
5. Attempt R19 construction with direct R10 segment `P1` but R17 segment `O2/P2`.
6. Construction must fail closed or route to governed correction because the two independently valid objects do not belong to one exact historical path.
7. Same Asset, same Product, same repository, same Release family, current deployment, technical equivalence, or commercial-equivalence claims may not silently reconcile the mismatch unless the governing contracts explicitly establish the required successor/equivalence authority.
8. Existing R10 or R17 historical identities must not be rewritten to manufacture agreement.

This is narrower than C09-02 because R19's frozen mission explicitly owns one exact composed historical path and R10→R17 already carries exact artifact binding. The fixture makes that composition executable; it does not establish an absent ownership relation.

### 4.6 Invalidation / re-review trigger

Any future R19 implementation that populates the direct R10 segment and R17 Offer segment from independent/current lookups without an exact-path consistency check reopens this seam. Any future R17 change that weakens exact artifact binding also requires re-review.

## 6. Phase C process notes carried forward

1. Complete-source negative evidence is strongest when the checked artifact otherwise enumerates neighboring consumed predicates in detail and the target seam remains absent across multiple natural representation surfaces.
2. Missing a named endpoint from one list is not sufficient by itself for `MISSING_REQUIRED_COMPOSITION`; the batch must establish that no equivalent representation or intentional mediator exists.
3. A seam can remain `CONSISTENT_CONSUMPTION` while still requiring an exact cross-object or exact-action fixture. Fixture need does not imply missing ownership.
4. Historical truth and current boundary eligibility remain separate. New cross-node predicates must preserve exact historical identity rather than turning boundary revalidation into mutable-current-state substitution.
5. Policy-version compatibility is part of identity-sensitive consequential reuse when the upstream authority contract makes materially changed policy semantically meaningful.

## 7. Endpoint-change invalidation rule

Any later change to an endpoint blob relied on by this batch requires edge-specific applicability review before inheriting these classifications or fixtures unchanged.

A third-party or neighboring artifact change does not automatically invalidate the endpoint classification, but any claimed mediator or composition that depends on that third party must be re-evaluated if its relevant semantics change.
