## 2. Governing reconciliation rules

### 2.1 Exact-once ownership

Each of the 57 primary Phase-H IDs appears exactly once as a primary lifecycle row.

A row may have several consumers. One recovery action may satisfy several rows. Neither fact changes primary ownership or arithmetic.

### 2.2 Source recovery and implementation authority are different questions

A source gap may reach a remediation-safe disposition without proving historical exactness where Phase H expressly permits:

- governed independent re-derivation;
- conservative implementation;
- safe fallback;
- continued provider/path non-authority;
- governed present-day adopted form with explicit historical non-claim.

No such disposition may be represented as recovered historical wording/form unless the source actually proves it.

### 2.3 No primary source is currently exhausted

Canonical H4 records zero `SOURCE_EXHAUSTED` rows.

Accordingly, this reconciliation never uses “source exhausted” as a closure premise.

### 2.4 Exactness must follow its consumer when correctness depends on exact form

H2 rows that can alter equality, identity, reference binding, uniqueness, cardinality, migration interpretation, registry membership, or fixture outcomes are attached to the consumer whose correctness depends on that exact form.

Pure historical-form/provenance packets remain traceability rows and do not block unrelated semantic implementation.

### 2.5 Phase-I handoff remains nine

Nine rows retain explicit Phase-I naming/string adjudication. Phase I may adopt governed present-day names where historical exact names remain unavailable, but must not launder adopted names into historical claims.

## 3. Lifecycle outcome vocabulary

Phase-H source-gap rows may resolve for remediation control into one of the following typed outcomes while preserving source-history truth:

- `SOURCE_RECOVERED` — stronger source directly answers the proposition;
- `GOVERNED_REDERIVATION_ADOPTED` — semantics independently re-derived and canonically adopted without historical wording claim;
- `CONSERVATIVE_RULE_ADOPTED` — finite/fail-closed conservative rule canonically adopted;
- `SAFE_FALLBACK_ADOPTED` — permissive optimization remains unavailable and safe slower/successor path is authoritative;
- `PROVIDER_PATH_NONAUTHORITATIVE` — provider/path remains disabled or non-authoritative until mapping evidence exists;
- `PROVIDER_MAPPING_VERIFIED` — concrete provider source set enumerated and mapping validated;
- `GOVERNED_CURRENT_FORM_ADOPTED` — exact historical form remains unproven but a present-day canonical representation/name/mechanism is explicitly adopted;
- `TRACEABILITY_OPEN_NONBLOCKING` — historical exactness remains open but does not block the semantically recovered authority path;
- `AMENDED_PENDING_RECHECK` — adopted/recovered proposition changed a downstream evidentiary basis and required rechecks are outstanding.
